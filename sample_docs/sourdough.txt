Sourdough Basics

A sourdough starter is a living culture of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Feed it equal weights of flour and water once a day at room temperature, or weekly if kept in the refrigerator.

The starter is ready to bake with when it doubles in size within four to six hours of feeding and smells pleasantly tangy rather than sharp.

Bulk fermentation takes four to eight hours at room temperature, depending on how warm the kitchen is. The dough should grow by about fifty percent and show bubbles along the edges.

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 240 degrees Celsius for twenty minutes with the lid on, then ten to fifteen minutes with the lid off to brown the crust.
