# The Solar System

The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust. The Sun holds more than 99 percent of its total mass.

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has almost no atmosphere. Venus is the hottest planet, with surface temperatures around 465 degrees Celsius, because of its dense carbon dioxide atmosphere.

Jupiter is the largest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm that has lasted for at least 300 years. Saturn is known for its rings, made mostly of water ice particles.

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun and takes about 165 Earth years to complete one orbit.
